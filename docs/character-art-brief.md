# Character art brief: Pranav + Simbha

Hi! I need two illustrated characters for my portfolio website: me and my cat Simbha.
On the site, I walk Simbha on a leash across a sunny landscape that turns from morning
to night as visitors scroll. The art will be animated in code, so the delivery format
below matters as much as the drawing.

## Reference photos
I'll attach 3 photos of me and 1 of Simbha.

## Style
- Flat, clean vector illustration: smooth shapes, soft shading (1 to 2 tones per colour), no outlines or thin dark outlines only.
- Friendly and polished, like a modern animated explainer, not pixel art and not hyper-realistic.
- It must look like me: face shape, hair, glasses, and beard are the priority.
- Side view, walking to the right (I'll mirror it in code for walking left).

## Me
- Hair: thick, dark black-brown, lots of volume on top, swept up and back, slightly messy.
- Glasses: black rectangular frames.
- Face: moustache plus light beard/stubble, warm medium-brown skin.
- Build: solid, average height.
- **Day outfit:** navy blue kurta (mandarin collar, light buttons, sleeves rolled to the forearm), dark trousers, white sneakers, brown leather crossbody laptop bag (strap across the chest, bag at the hip).
- **Night outfit:** the same, but a black hoodie instead of the kurta (bag stays).
- One hand slightly forward holding a leash.

## Simbha
- Fluffy long-haired Persian-style cat with a round, flat face.
- White body, legs, chest and face with silver-grey tabby patches on the back and top of the head.
- Big fluffy tail, amber eyes, pink nose.
- Blue collar with a small red bell.

## What to deliver
**Animations (side view, facing right):**
1. Me: walk cycle, 8 frames, day outfit.
2. Me: walk cycle, 8 frames, night outfit.
3. Me: idle, 2 to 4 frames (breathing or small blink), day and night.
4. Simbha: walk cycle, 8 frames.
5. Simbha: idle, 2 to 4 frames (tail swish).
6. Simbha: happy hop, 4 to 6 frames (when clicked).

**Nice to have:** me waving (for the contact section), and me sitting at a desk with a laptop, Simbha on the desk (for the night "home" scene).

**Format:**
- Preferred: **layered SVG** (head, torso, each arm, each leg, bag, tail as separate named groups) so the animation can be done in code. Or, alternatively:
- **PNG frames with transparent background**, every frame on the same canvas size (me: 600 × 1000 px, Simbha: 600 × 400 px), feet on the same baseline row in every frame.
- Please mark (or tell me the x/y of) **my leash hand** and **Simbha's collar** in each frame, so the leash can be drawn between them.
- Source files (AI / Figma / Procreate) included, with full rights for me to use on my website.
